# Code of Conduct

Treat participants with respect, welcome different experiences, and keep feedback
focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and sharing
someone's private information without permission are unacceptable.

Participation follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
including its
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
This applies to the repository, its discussions, and spaces where participants
represent the project.

## Report privately

Email [info@devdocs.work](mailto:info@devdocs.work),
[DevDocs' published contact](https://devdocs.work/terms#contact), and identify this
repository and the conduct concern. Describe what happened, provide relevant
links, and state how you would like to be contacted. Share only the personal
information needed to explain the concern. Identify any conflict involving a
maintainer when requesting review.
